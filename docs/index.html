Intraday breakout experiment

A separate paper test of an opening-range breakout on Nifty 200 stocks. It has not shown a reliable profit after charges; it keeps running so the evidence can build up.

Trades

How the intraday test trades
  1. Which stocks: at 9:25 it ranks the Nifty 200 by how unusual the early volume was and follows the top 3.
  2. Buy or short: price up over the first 10 minutes means buy only; price down means short only.
  3. Entry: when price trades beyond the high or low of those 10 minutes. No entry after 14:30.
  4. Stop loss: fixed at 25% of the stock's average daily range from the entry price.
  5. Exit: no target. Open trades close at 15:05.
Paper money only: no real orders are placed. Results include brokerage, STT, stamp duty, exchange fees, GST and DP charges. This page is a record of an experiment, not investment advice.
