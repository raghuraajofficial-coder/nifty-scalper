How this trades
  1. Which stocks: at 9:20 it ranks the Nifty 200 by how unusual the first 5 minutes of volume were, compared with each stock's last 14 sessions, and follows the top 3.
  2. Buy or short: the first candle (9:15 to 9:20) decides. Green candle: buy only. Red candle: short only.
  3. Entry: buy when price trades above that first candle's high; short when it trades below its low. No entry after 14:30.
  4. Stop loss: one fixed stop per trade, placed 25% of the stock's average daily range away from the entry price. It does not move.
  5. Profit: no target. A winning trade is held until 15:05 and closed before the broker's 15:10 square-off.
  6. Size: each trade risks 0.5% of capital (about ₹500) if stopped, with total positions capped at 4 times capital.
